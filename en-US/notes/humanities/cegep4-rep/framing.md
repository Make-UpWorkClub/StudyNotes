# framing

Act of choosing the context and emphasis of facts to produce meaning

- Facts mean nothing on their own.
- Meaning is produced when facts are put into context.
- Job of the journalists
- Good framing:
- Bad framing:
	- Media bias
	- Rationalism: distorts the truth
	- Virtue ethics: unjust
	- `E.g.` protest framed as riot
