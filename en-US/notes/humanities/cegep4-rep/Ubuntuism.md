# Ubuntuism

Communitarian philosophy in [[South Africa]]

- Derived from the maxim "I am because of others"
- Collective African consciousness
- Emphasis on individual participation in a collective life
- Individuality defined by and in service of community
- Virtue ethics: compassion, harmony, reciprocity, care, justice
- Disadvantages:
	- Can be hijacked for political purposes just like nationalism
	- Only works if everyone buys in
