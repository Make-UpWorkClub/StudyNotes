# complex ion

Charged ion consisting of a metal ion surrounded by **ligands**

Common ligands:

- $\ce{H_2o}$
- $\ce{NH_3}$
- $\ce{CI^-}$
- $\ce{CN^-}$

> [!example] $\ce{Al(H_2O)_6^{3 +}}$

> [!abstract] coordination number
> Number of attached ligands
