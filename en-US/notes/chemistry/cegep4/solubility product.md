# solubility product

Product of *maximal* concentration of ions at equilibrium

$$
K_{sp} = \prod[\text{Ions}]^n
$$

- Excludes solids
- Constant at any temperature

> [!info] It is only possible to compare solubility product of salts that produce the same number of ions.
