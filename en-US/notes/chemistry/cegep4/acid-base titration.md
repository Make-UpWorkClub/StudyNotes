# acid-base titration

[[Titration]] with [[acid-base reaction]]

## Examples

> What volume of 0.01M NaOH must be added to 1L of 0.05M HOCl to achieve a pH of 8? $K_a = 3.5 \cdot 10^{-8}$

$$
\begin{align}
pK_a & = -\log 3.5 \cdot 10^{-8} = 7.46 \\
pH = pK_a + \log \frac{A^-}{HA} \\
8 & = 7.46 - \log \frac{x}{0.05 - x} \\
x & = 0.0388\pu{mol} \\
 \\
\frac{0.0388\pu{mol}}{0.01\pu{M}} & = 3.8\pu{L}
\end{align}
$$
