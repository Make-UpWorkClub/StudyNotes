# entropy

[[State function]] of disorder in a system


$$
\begin{align}
[S] & = \pu{\frac{J}{K}} \\
\Delta S & = -\frac{\Delta H}{T} \\
\Delta S_{uni} & = \Delta S_{surr} + \Delta S
\end{align}
$$

- Can be measured as an absolute value
- Typically, entropy increases if more gas molecules are formed in a reaction.
