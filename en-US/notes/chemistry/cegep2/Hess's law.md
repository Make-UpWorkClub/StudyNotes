# Hess's Law

Change in [[enthalpy]] is the same whether a [[reaction]] happens in one step or in multiple steps.

$$
\Delta H_T = \sum a_p\Delta H_p - \sum a_r\Delta H_r
$$

Can be extended to other state functions like [[entropy]] and [[Gibbs free energy]]:

$$
\begin{align}
\Delta S^\circ & = \sum n_pS^\circ_p - \sum n_rS^\circ_r \\
\Delta G^\circ & = \sum v_p\Delta G^\circ_p - \sum v_r\Delta G^\circ_r
\end{align}
$$

## Consequences

1. Reversed reaction has opposite $\Delta H$ sign.
2. Quantity of reaction is proportional to $\Delta H$.
