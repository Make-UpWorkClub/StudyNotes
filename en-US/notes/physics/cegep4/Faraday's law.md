# Faraday's law

[[Electric induction]] in a circuit by change in [[magnetic flux]]

$$
\epsilon = \left\lvert  \frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t}  \right\rvert
$$

## Examples

> A 16 cm × 16 cm square loop lies in the xy-plane. The magnetic field in this region of space is 𝐵 = (0.31𝑡̂ 𝚤 + 0.51𝑡! 1𝑘) 𝑇, where 𝑡 is in seconds.

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt} & = \frac{d}{dt}(\vec{B} \cdot \vec{A}) \\
& = \frac{d}{dt}(0.013056t^2) \\
& = 0.026112t \\
\\

\end{align}
$$

> a) What is the magnitude of the emf induced in the loop at 𝑡 = 0.5 𝑠?

> b) What is the magnitude of the emf induced in the loop at 𝑡 = 1.0 𝑠?

> c) If the loop has a total resistance of 5Ω, what is the value of the induced current at 𝑡 = 1.0 𝑠 ?
