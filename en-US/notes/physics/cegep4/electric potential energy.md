# electric potential energy

[[énergie potentielle|Potential energy]] created by [[electric force]]
Antiderivative of electric force

$$
\begin{align}
U_\text{elec} & = qV \\
\Delta U_\text{elec} & = q\Delta V \\
\Delta U_\text{elec} & = - \int_i^f \vec{F} \cdot \mathrm{d} \vec{r}
\end{align}
$$

> [!important] Particles naturally move in the direction of decreasing $U$.
