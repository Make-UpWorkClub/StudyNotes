# straight wire

[[Magnetic field]] of straight wire

$$
B = \frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi a}
$$

- $a$: distance from wire

[[Magnetic force]] on straight wire

$$
\vec{F}_B = I\vec{v} \times \vec{B}
$$

- $\vec{B}$: external magnetic field
