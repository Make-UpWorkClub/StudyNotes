# Coulomb's law

[[Electric force]] between two [[point charge|point charges]]

$$
-\vec{F_{21}} = \vec{F_{12}} = \frac{k_eq_1q_2}{r^2}\hat{r_{12}}
$$

- $k_e = 8.99 \cdot 10^9\pu{\frac{Nm^2}{C^2}}$: **electrostatic constant**
- $\hat{r_{12}}$: unit vector from charge 1 to charge 2
