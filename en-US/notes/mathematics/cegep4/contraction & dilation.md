# contraction & dilation

[[en-US/notes/mathematics/cegep4/linear transformation|linear transformation]] corresponding to contraction or dilation

$$
\begin{align}
T(x, y) = T(kx, ky) \\
[T] = \begin{bmatrix}
k & 0 \\
0 & k
\end{bmatrix} \\
W_{k} & = \mathbb{R}^2
\end{align}
$$
