# projection theorem

Let $W$ be a finite dimensional subspace of an inner product space $V$,
Then any vector $\vec{v} \in V$ can be expressed in only one way as

$$
\vec{v} = \vec{w}_{1} + \vec{w}_{2}
$$

Where $\vec{w}_{1} \in W$ is the [[projection vectorielle|projection]] of $\vec{v}$ onto $W$ and $\vec{w}_{2} \in W^\perp$ is the projection of $\vec{v}$ onto $W^\perp$.
