# angle

[[vecteur|Vector]] operation
The angle between two vectors $\vec{u}, \vec{v}$ in a real [[inner product]] space is arccos of [[norme|normalized]] inner product of $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$:

$$
\theta(\vec{u}, \vec{v}) = \arccos \frac{\langle \vec{u}, \vec{v} \rangle}{||\vec{u}|| \cdot ||\vec{v}||}
$$
