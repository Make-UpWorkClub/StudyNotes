# angle

The angle between two vectors $\vec{u}, \vec{v}$ in a real [[inner product]] space is arccos of [[norme|normalized]] inner product of $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$:

$$
\theta = \arccos \frac{\langle \vec{u}, \vec{v} \rangle}{||\vec{u}|| \cdot ||\vec{v}||}
$$

> [!abstract] orthogonality
> Property of two vectors $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{w}$ such that $\theta(\vec{v}, \vec{w}) = \frac{\pi}{2}$. In other words,
> 
> $$
> \langle \vec{v}, \vec{w} \rangle = 0
> $$
> 
> By convention, $\vec{0}$ is orthogonal to any vector.

## Examples

> Let $V = M_{22}$ with standard inner product
