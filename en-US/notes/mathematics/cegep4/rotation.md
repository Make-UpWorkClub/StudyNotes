# rotation

[[en-US/notes/mathematics/cegep4/linear transformation|Linear transformation]] corresponding to rotation counter-clockwise

$$
\begin{align}
T(x, y) & = (\cos\theta x - \sin\theta y, \sin\theta x + \cos\theta y) \\
[T] & = \begin{bmatrix}
\cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\
\sin\theta & \cos\theta
\end{bmatrix} \\
2\pi|\theta - \pi \implies W_{-1} & = \mathbb{R}^2 \\
2\pi|\theta \implies W_1 & = \mathbb{R}^2
\end{align}
$$
