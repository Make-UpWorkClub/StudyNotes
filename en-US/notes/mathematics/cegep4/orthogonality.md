# orthogonality

Property of two vectors $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{w}$ such that the [[angle]] between them is $\frac{\pi}{2}$
`Sym.` $\perp$

$$
\begin{align}
\theta(\vec{v}, \vec{w}) & = \frac{\pi}{2} \\
\langle \vec{v}, \vec{w} \rangle & = 0
\end{align}
$$

- ==By convention, $\vec{0}$ is orthogonal to any vector.==
